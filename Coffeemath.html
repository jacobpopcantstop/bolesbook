Answer Key

Teacher Copy

1. The Breakfast Run

  • Step A: $2.75 × 2 = $5.50
  • Step B: $4.25 + $5.50 = $9.75
  • Step C: $20.00 - $9.75 = $10.25

2. Splitting the Bill

  • Step A: $4.75 × 4 = $19.00
  • Step B: $3.20 × 2 = $6.40
  • Step C: $19.00 + $6.40 = $25.40
  • Step D: $25.40 ÷ 4 = $6.35

3. Weekly Budget

  • Step A: $2.50 × 5 = $12.50
  • Step B: $12.50 + $4.25 = $16.75
  • Step C: $20.00 - $16.75 = $3.25
  • Answer: UNDER budget by $3.25

4. Challenge: The Teacher's Order

  • Croissants: 12 × $3.20 = $38.40
  • Lattes: 12 × $4.25 = $51.00
  • Subtotal: $38.40 + $51.00 = $89.40
  • Final Total: $89.40 - $10.00 = $79.40
